# MemeVibe

**[MemeVibe](https://memevibe.app/)** puts you inside trending short videos. Pick a clip from the weekly trending list, upload one photo of yourself, and get the video back with you in it.

It's a full [AI body swap](https://memevibe.app/), not just a face pasted on top: hair, clothing and body shape follow the original motion and timing.

👉 **Try it: [memevibe.app](https://memevibe.app/)**

## Features

- **[Weekly trending templates](https://memevibe.app/trends)**: the template list is refreshed every week
- **One photo in, finished clip out**: no filming or editing skills needed
- **Full body swap**: replaces the whole person, not only the face
- **[Pay per video](https://memevibe.app/pricing)**: credit packs, no subscription

## How it works

1. Pick a [trending template](https://memevibe.app/trends)
2. Upload a clear, well-lit photo of yourself
3. Download your clip and share it

## Tips for best results

- Use a photo where your face and most of your body are visible
- Avoid heavy filters, sunglasses and tight crops
- Only upload photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed to it

More guides and trend breakdowns on the [MemeVibe blog](https://memevibe.app/blog).
